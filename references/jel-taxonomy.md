# JEL classification

JEL codes are a coarse topic signal. Extract them only from an explicitly labeled JEL field, not any isolated letter-plus-two-digits token. Relevant examples for a corporate finance and firm-boundaries monitor may include G21, G32, G34, L14, L22, L23, L24, D23, and O16. These are examples, not an automatic inclusion rule.

Keep user topic relevance, methods, data, mechanism, and source priority separate from JEL. Absence of a JEL field is not evidence that a paper lies outside economics.
