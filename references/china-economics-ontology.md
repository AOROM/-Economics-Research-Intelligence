# China economics context

`research_radar/data/china.yaml` groups enterprise types, financial institutions, policy terms, and named datasets. It helps tag the context of a Chinese empirical paper. A matched term is evidence that the term appears in title or abstract; it does not prove sample design, treatment assignment, or policy identification.

For a policy study, distinguish the named policy, stated timing, treatment group, control group, geographic scope, and identification method. The executable extractor stores source excerpts when present and leaves the rest unfilled. Check the paper before writing causal interpretation.
