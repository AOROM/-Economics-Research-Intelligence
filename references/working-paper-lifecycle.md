# Working-paper and journal lifecycle

An NBER, CEPR, RePEc, or SSRN appearance may precede a revision, forthcoming notice, online journal article, and final issue. Keep each official URL and observation as a `versions` event of one canonical work when identity evidence is strong.

Identity order: DOI exact match; stable source ID or official URL; exact or very close normalized title with overlapping authors. Ambiguous near matches are saved as `possible_links` and must be checked before merging. A common title alone is never enough. Publication status changes remain visible in the weekly report but do not count as a second new work.

Keep four times conceptually distinct: the monitor's first observation, the first public version date reported by a source, the current observed version date, and the journal publication date. Do not infer a missing date from Crossref deposits or a feed item's collection time.
