# Bilingual economic concepts

The executable vocabulary lives in `research_radar/data/concepts.yaml`. Each concept has Chinese and English aliases. The seed set covers firm boundaries, financial frictions, investment, and mergers. Extend it only with genuinely related economic expressions; do not use the list as a mechanical translation dictionary.

Alias matches are retrieval and ranking evidence. A word match does not establish that a paper studies a mechanism. Verify mechanism claims in abstract or full text and preserve the source sentence. Review false positives and missed synonyms against a small labeled set when adding vocabulary.
